## Minute 1:00 – 2:15 | Scenario Lab & Infeasibility Diagnostics (Tab 2)

1. **Interactive Controls:**
   - Switch to **Tab 2: 🧪 Scenario Lab**.
   - Show the operational sliders: Demand Surge, Inventory Ratio, Vehicle Fleet Capacity, Transport Throttle, and Minimum Service Coverage ($\alpha_{\min}$).
2. **Trigger Real Optimization:**
   - Set Demand Surge to `+20%`, Policy to `Deterministic MILP (HiGHS)`.
   - Click **🚀 Run Multi-Echelon Optimization**.
   - Note the solver execution: status changes to `Optimal` with sub-second runtime (~0.4s) and 0.0% optimality gap.
3. **Inspect Output Analytics:**
   - Cost Decomposition Waterfall: Shows exact breakdown of Transport ($), Route Activation fees ($150 + $1.20/km), Vehicle fees ($200/dispatch), and Recourse Shortage penalties.
   - Fill Rate Distribution: Confirms that clinical severity weights protected Trauma Centers while fulfilling lower-priority demand.
4. **Demonstrate Infeasibility & Relaxation Analysis:**
   - Change Available Inventory Ratio to `40%` and Minimum Service Coverage to `0.85`.
   - Click **🚀 Run Multi-Echelon Optimization**.
   - Point out that AEGIS **never crashes or fakes optimality**: the solver explicitly flags `INFEASIBLE TARGET`.
   - Show the amber **Fairness Relaxation Analyzer card**: displays maximum achievable coverage $\alpha^* \approx 40\%$ and calculates the exact physical inventory shortfall required to achieve the requested target.
